# Worldbuilding workflow

The Archmage Scribe supports two complementary activities:

1. `idea-explore`: develop and examine ideas, concepts, rules, histories, and implications.
2. `fantasy-write`: produce texts and narratives grounded in the fictional world's canon.

The workflow is iterative, not linear. Either skill may be requested independently. Writing may expose gaps or contradictions that lead back to exploration; exploration may produce material ready to be written.

For every task, consult relevant canonical documents from the configured local repository. Retrieve only material relevant to the current instruction. Portuguese canonical documents are authoritative; AI-oriented summaries and indexes may help locate sources but do not override them.

Maintain a distinction between:

- objective facts about the fictional world;
- what a particular inhabitant knows;
- what that inhabitant believes;
- interpretations held by a school, culture, or tradition;
- matters that remain unknown or disputed.

New proposals do not automatically become canon. Identify their relationship to existing canon and leave decisions about canonical changes to the user.

All generated documents remain proposals in the session. The agent never writes to the content repository.
